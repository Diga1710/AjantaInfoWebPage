How to Reach Ajanta Caves

📍 Location

Ajanta Caves are located in the Aurangabad district of Maharashtra, India. They are around 100 km from Aurangabad city.

🚆 By Train

The nearest railway station is Jalgaon Junction (about 60 km away). Regular buses and taxis are available from Jalgaon to Ajanta Caves.

✈️ By Air

The nearest airport is Aurangabad Airport. From Aurangabad, tourists can reach Ajanta Caves by bus or taxi.

🚌 By Road

Ajanta Caves are well connected by road. MSRTC buses and private taxis are available from Aurangabad and Jalgaon.
